    This is a mock-up. Nothing here talks to the real game. Distances and speeds are to the original's scale: a sector is 10,000 units, you orbit within 250, and warp 10 crosses a sector in about 20 seconds. The Star Cruiser's 3,000-ton hold, item weights, neutral-zone planet names and the torpedo lock formula are the original game's too. Planets, owners, prices, damage and AI are made up.
