## 2. HIGHEST-IMPACT FIXES, RANKED

Ranked by effect on the owner's two stated goals: **new players must survive**, and **new players must be able to hunt and kill**.

### #1 — `cbearing` must return a SIGNED bearing (−180..180)
**Change:** add `cbearing(from, to, heading)` in `physics-math.ts`: `let b = normalizeHeading(headingToward(dx,dy) - heading); if (b > 180) b -= 360; return b;`. Route `scan.handler.ts:79-80`, `helpers/scantab.ts:59-60`, `scan-announce.ts:47-48` and `nav.handler.ts:23-27` through it. `nav` must additionally pass `ship.heading` — it currently computes an **absolute compass bearing and never subtracts heading at all** (`GECMDS.C:5143` passes `warsptr->heading`).
**Canon:** `GELIB.C:142-166`, fold at `:160-161`. Consumer `GEFUNCS.C:1941` `valdegree` accepts only −180..180.
**Effect — LETHALITY, decisive.** The new-player kill loop is `scan sh <name>` → read bearing → `pha <bearing> <focus>`. We print `300` for any target on the port side (half of all targets); `phaser.handler.ts:120` then rejects it with "number out of range −180 to 180" and nothing hints at subtracting 360. **Half of all attempted shots are currently unfireable.** Same broken number feeds the "you are being scanned from bearing N" warning a hunted pilot uses to pick an escape heading.

### #2 — Restore the shipped weapon/damage defaults in `game.config.json`
**Change (11 values):** `PDAMMAX 25→50`, `PFIRDST 3→7`, `HPDAMMAX 200→50`, `HPFIRDST 1→9`, `TDAMMAX 100→35`, `MDAMMAX 100→25`, `IDAMMAX 100→50`, `MNDAMMAX 150→75`, `TORPSPED 500→2441`, `MISLSPED 300→1212`, `MISENGFC 10→100`. Mirror each into the `SYSOP_OPTIONS` defaults at `game-config.ts:69-85,100`.
**Canon:** `MBMGEMSG.MSG:303,309,324,333,339,349,356,379,403,411,417,425`.
**Effect — SURVIVAL, decisive.** Worked numbers at the current values:
- Hyper-phaser at 2 sectors: `dd = 0.5`. Canon `50 × 0.5⁹` truncates to **0**. Ours `200 × 0.5 = 100` base, ×Mark-10 phasrtype ÷ tonfact ≈ **937 damage — instant kill** (destruction at ≥100).
- Normal phaser at 5 sectors, Mark-10 (`disfact = 60000`, `dd = 0.1667`): canon `0.1667⁷ = 3.6e-6` (wasted shot). Ours `0.1667³ = 0.0046` — **~1300× more damage**, so phasers snipe from beyond the range at which a new pilot can see the shooter.
- Unshielded torpedo: canon 17–35 hull (3–4 hits to kill). Ours 50–100 — **one-shot**.
- Point-blank mine: canon ≤75. Ours ≤150 — unconditional kill regardless of hull class.
- Ion cannon volley: canon 25–50. Ours 50–100 — orbiting a garrisoned colony unshielded is instant death.
Note this fix also **raises the player's own phaser** (25→50), the one weapon currently *weakened* — the errors do not push one direction, which is the fingerprint of bounds-picking rather than tuning.

**Canon:** `GECMDS.C:3655 ratio = (left1*100UL)/left2;`. Our own fighter branch at `:178` already does `(left1/left2)*100`, proving it is a slip.
**Effect — LETHALITY, decisive.** 500 troops landed on a 400-troop garrison yields `ratio = 1` here instead of `125`. Defenders take **zero** casualties however many troops land; no planet items are destroyed; the owner is **never alerted and never mailed** (both gated on the same value). Canon triggers casualties at 3% of the garrison. Taking a planet by troops currently needs >2× the garrison rather than 2% — **ground invasion, the normal route from a first kill to a first income stream, is effectively non-functional.**

## 3. OWNER DECISIONS REQUIRED

**D1 — `UNIVMAX`: canon 300, ours 100.**
Canon ships a 601×601 galaxy; `game.config.json:25` runs 201×201 with a written AI-density rationale. It *is* a sysop option (`N 10 32000`), so this is a legitimate tune, not a defect. **Recommend: keep 100, but fix `game-config.ts:64`'s `default` to 300 and record the deviation in `docs/DECISIONS.md`** — the current comment misattributes 10 to the C source, which is what licensed the drift.
*Coupled:* `SCAN_LO_PROJECTION_MULTIPLIER = 3` (`constants.ts:95`) vs canon's `*10.0` (`GECMDS.C:2668`). Its JSDoc justifies 3 by calling `MAXX`/`MAXY` the galaxy size — they are the character-grid divisor. At UNIVMAX 100 a canon ×10 does **not** hand a starter Interceptor a free full-galaxy map. **Recommend: set it to 10** and correct the JSDoc.

**D2 — `PLANTOCK`: canon 360 minutes, ours hard-coded 1800 seconds (30 min).**
Our JSDoc claims "canonical default 30 minutes"; the shipped default is 360. We run planet economies **12× faster than the original** — colonies mature in hours instead of days, which inflates planet-derived score against combat-derived score, the exact ratio 3.2c retuned. But 6-hour ticks in a modern web game with daily logins may feel dead.
**Options:** (a) canon 360; (b) a documented tune around 120 (still 3× canon speed, 4× slower than today); (c) leave 30.
**Recommend (b)**, with `PLANTOCK` added to `SYSOP_OPTIONS` (min 1, max 32760, default **360**) and 120 recorded as a deliberate deviation in `game.config.json`. This is the fidelity-vs-playability call with the largest economy-wide blast radius — flag it before touching scoring tests.

**D3 — `UNIVWRAP`: canon ships **NO**. We always wrap.**
With wrap off, `GEFUNCS.C:651-705` pins the ship at ±(univmax−2), calls `telezip()` which **zeroes speed and speed2b and adds TELEDAM (17)**. We instead teleport the ship across the galaxy at full speed for free — inverting a core escape/pursuit mechanic. Note `TELEDAM = 17` is defined at `constants.ts:224`, balance-tested, and **read by no runtime code** — a pinned number nothing consumes.
**Tension:** canon's hard wall punishes a fleeing new player; wrap is friendlier.
**Recommend: implement the mechanic and the option, default to canon `NO`.** Running for the edge should be a dead end, not a free jump — and the free jump currently benefits Cybertrons chasing players as much as players fleeing.

**D4 — "Men don't eat" (inherited original bug).**
`GEPLANET.C:221-230` debits food only for troops, then starves men against that same stock. A colony with no garrison currently consumes zero food forever — a perpetual-motion machine — and food, the item the user guide sends new players to Tahanian Station to buy, is nearly worthless.
**Options:** (a) fix (debit `floor(men/100)` before the starvation test) — restores the supply-chain loop but sharply raises colony logistics burden; (b) leave as canon-bug.
**Recommend (a), but only alongside a food-production retune**, and only after D2 is settled — the two multiply. Owner has ruled bugs are not kept, so (a); the caveat is sequencing, not principle.

**D5 — Gold base price: 100 (ours) vs 1000. Canon cannot arbitrate.**
`grep ITMPR MBMGEMSG.MSG` returns **zero hits** — the shipped `.MSG` predates `GEMAIN.C:569`, so there is no canon default for any item base price. Evidence is wiki-only and split: `sysop-options.md:306` (a direct option-block transcription from a later `.MSG`) and `colonizing-planets.md:49` both say **1000**; `items.md:24` says 100 — and that is the same summary row that gets gold's *weight* demonstrably wrong.
**Recommend: 1000**, on the strength of the two agreeing sources against the one proven wrong, with an in-code comment recording that this rests on wiki transcription, not canon. At 100, gold shipped to a colony converts to planetary cash at one tenth the intended rate and the Zygor markup is a tenth of canon — removing most of the trade loop a new player is meant to use to fund a first real ship.

**D6 — Sysopian Death Star numbering: 41 (ours) vs 34 (canon's command).**
`GECMDS.C:412` prints `i+1` = **34** and `:4562` parses `atoi(margv[2])-1`; the shipped `MBMGESHP.MSG:7585` labels the block `S41TYPE`. Both defensible.
**Recommend: keep 41** (matches the data file, less confusing) and document it — but note it is moot once the purchase gate lands, since canon makes the hull unreachable either way. **Independently required regardless of D6:** bound both the listing (`new-ship.handler.ts:98`) and the purchase (`:143`) to `classNumber < 21`, per `cyb_class` at `GECMDS.C:378` and `:4562-4564`. Any player with 32M credits can currently buy the admin-only hull, and it is **advertised in the `new ship` menu to every pilot from day one**.

**D7 — Established-player caps: `MAXPLNTS 256→20`, `MAXPLRS 256→30`, `MAXSHIPS 10→8`, `MAILDAYS 7→3`, `TOOCLOSE 3000→2500`.**
All favour newcomers at the expense of incumbents. `MAXPLNTS` is the one that matters: canon's 20-planet cap is **what leaves unclaimed worlds for players arriving after the first wave.** At 256, one established player can own the entire habitable galaxy and a latecomer finds no path into the planet economy.
**Recommend: take canon on all five.** `TOOCLOSE 3000` currently favours the player marginally; going to 2500 is a small survival cost, acceptable for consistency.
